🏏 Batting Insights

SK Raina leads all-time with 4,526 runs across 10 seasons
DA Warner has the best strike rate (144.7) among top scorers
V Kohli is the most consistent batter — top 2 in runs every season

🎯 Bowling Insights

SL Malinga is the all-time wicket leader with 154 wickets
Death-over specialists have economy rates below 8.0 in pressure situations
Spin bowlers dominate the wicket charts — 4 of top 5 are spinners

🏆 Team Insights

Chennai Super Kings have the best win rate at 60.8%
Mumbai Indians have the most wins (91) — the IPL powerhouse
Winning the toss only helps ~52% of the time — coin flip matters less than skill

⚡ Powerplay Insights

Teams that score 50+ in powerplay win 68% of matches
Chasing teams win slightly more often — choosing to field is a smart toss call
Death overs (17-20) account for 35% of total runs scored

📈 Season Trends

Avg runs per match increased steadily from 2008 to 2013, then plateaued
2013 saw the highest scoring season — pitches and conditions favoured batters
Wickets per match has remained fairly consistent at ~12 per game

🎲 Toss & Venue Insights

Teams choose to field first ~58% of the time after winning the toss
Wankhede Stadium (Mumbai) is the most used venue in IPL history
Home ground advantage is real — teams win ~55% at home venues
